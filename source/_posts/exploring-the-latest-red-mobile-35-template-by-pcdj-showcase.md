## Exploring the Latest: RED Mobile 3.5 Template by PCDJ Showcase

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/rm35skinpreview-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/rm35skinpreview-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "rm35skinpreview-coverimage")

An upgrade to the RED Mobile 3 **DJ mixing software** platform is due out this November and among various other added features, it will include a new [graphical user interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphical%5Fuser%5Finterface) (skin). As with all updates the RED Mobile 3.5 update will be free for existing users.

3.5 will include a few new browser features, requested changes to the sidelist/karaoke singers list, under-the-hood performance improvements, support for freshly mapped [DJ Controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and a couple (should be!) welcomed surprises. It’s a DJ software’s GUI, however, that’s the “_face_” of the product – and while it’s the “_guts_” of the app that count the most when DJing, no one wants to stare at an interface for hours on end that’s out-dated or otherwise displeasing to the eye.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT RED MOBILE 3 (CURRENT VERSION) ![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/newredmobilelogo.png?fit=255%2C300&ssl=1 "newredmobilelogo")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

We previous posted a preview of the DEX 3.5 GUI [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) — and DEX 3 users will get their hands on the upgrade with new skin in the next week or two. The upcoming RED Mobile 3.5 skin showcases a similar “_flat design_“, but uses similar overall aesthetics as previous versions, so RED Mobile 3 users will feel right at home while mixing.

Here’s a (small) preview of the upcoming RED Mobile 3.5 skin, which will also dynamically fit any display’s screen resolution without any loss in graphical quality. _Like what you see? Think it’s ugly? We would love to hear from you! Feel free to post your thoughts in the comment section below!_
