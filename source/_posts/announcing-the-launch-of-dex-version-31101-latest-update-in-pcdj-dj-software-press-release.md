## DJ Software and Karaoke Software designers Digital 1 Audio have raised the bar for Disc Jockey’s and venues with their DEX 3.11 all-in-one entertainment software update.

Digital 1 Audio, Inc ([http://www.pcdj.com](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)), pioneers of professional DJ, video mixing, and karaoke software products for DJs and karaoke hosts since 1998, launched version 3.11 of their flagship ‘Do Everything’ [DEX 3 DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). The free upgrade rounds out the live performance software’s features that target the requirements of the new Mobile DJ, providing industry-leading value for it’s users.

“We’ve gone well beyond creating simple DJ Mixing Software. DEX 3 is live performance entertainment software designed for today’s versatile Mobile DJ, and a great fit for venue installation,” says Digital 1 Audio’s General Manager Ryan Sherr. “The value in having one software, one robust product, that provides all the tools a DJ needs to perform with music, music videos, and host karaoke, with or without one of the [100+ supported DJ Controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), is tremendous. In DEX 3.11 we’ve fine-tuned karaoke support, including support for the [Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription service](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), which provides in-app access to over 15,000 HD quality karaoke songs. Karaoke hosts will also enjoy the benefit of being able to pre-adjust the key of karaoke tracks when adding them to the singer rotation list.” Sherr Continues “we’re most excited about bringing to DEX 3.11 is support for ‘[Shaders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shader)’, which can now be added to DEX 3 to create beat-aware on-screen visualizations. Shader support adds to DEX 3’s already rich set of video mixing features and allow DJs to [display stunning on-screen visual](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jKDgSaaiJFE)s when just mixing audio tracks.” DEX 3.11 also adds polish to existing features with various under-the-hood performance improvements.

**Here are notable features and/or improvements included in DEX 3 version 3.11:** 

* Added Visualizations (using [ShaderToy.com](http://www.shadertoy.com) shaders) – DJ can add new shaders from ShaderToy.com, rename, remove them, etc
* DJ can pre-adjust key / apply key change in the karaoke singer rotation list
* DJ can add tracks from History that have a singer name and key change to the karaoke rotation list (the saved Singer and Key change will be used)
* Added “Always show shaders on top of everything” setting (Video preferences tab)
* Automatically hide/show shader in case the deck does/doesn’t have video – DJ needs to select “0 singers” in the Karaoke preferences or have an audio-only track playing if they want to see the shader; or, can simply use the new “Always show shaders on top of everything” setting

DEX 3.11’s manufacturer’s suggested retail price is $179.

The optional in-app commercially legal Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription is[free to try for 7 days](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and $99.99-a-month thereafter.

**Recommended System Requirements:**

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS: WINDOWS

* Windows: 7 / Windows 8 / Windows 8.1 / Windows 10
* 2.5GHz Intel Core i3/i5/i7
* 4 GIG RAM or Better (If you have more than 120,000 songs we’d recommend at 8GB or more)
* Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or Intel HD 4000 Series or better)
* DirectX/ASIO Compatible Multi-Channel Audio Interface (and/or USB audio interface)
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS: MAC

* Mac: OSX 10.9 or greater
* 2.5GHz Intel Core i3/i5/i7
* 4 GIG RAM or Better (If you have more than 120,000 songs we’d recommend at least 8GB)
* Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or Intel HD 4000/Iris Series or better)
* Multi-channel Core Audio soundcard (and/or USB audio interface)
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive

**Important Note:** The above are recommendations for standard 480p (SD) Video Mixing:

* For 720p HD videos: At least 4GB of RAM, 8GB recommended, a video card with at least 512MB dedicated video RAM
* For 1080p/1080i full HD videos: At least 4GB of RAM, 8GB recommended, a video card with at least 1GB dedicated video RAM
