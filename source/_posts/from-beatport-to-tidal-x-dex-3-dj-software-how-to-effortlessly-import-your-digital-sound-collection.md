## From Beatport to Tidal X DEX 3 DJ Software: How to Effortlessly Import Your Digital Sound Collection

[![TIDAL Importing Playlists from other Streaming Services](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/tidal-import-playlists-1.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/tidal-import-playlists-1.jpg?fit=1030%2C541&ssl=1 "TIDAL Importing Playlists from other Streaming Services")

TIDAL streaming support just dropped in [DEX 3.17](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), providing DJs with in-app access to their massive catalog of 80 million plus tracks and 350,000 plus music videos.

[Learn More About DEX 3.17 with TIDAL ![PCDJ DEX 3](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/dex3-macbook-tidal.png?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "PCDJ DEX 3")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

With [TIDAL](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)‘s unique library, curated playlists for DJs, high-fidelity playback, and lightning quick streaming – it’s surely going to be a top subscription service for [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJs.

But, what if you’ve been subscribed to another streaming service such as Spotify or Deezer, and have taken considerable time and effort to create playlists? You just don’t want to start from scratch!

Don’t fret, with [Tune My Music](https://www.tunemymusic.com/?tidal=true#step1) or [Soundiiz](https://soundiiz.com/partner/tidal) transferring your playlists from your previous streaming service of choice to TIDAL is a cinch.

As TIDAL puts it:

> “We know it takes time and energy to curate the perfect music library. Transfer your old music library and playlists to TIDAL with just a few easy steps.”

Click the button below to visit [Tune My Music](https://www.tunemymusic.com/?tidal=true#step1) and begin the conversion process so you can utilize your painstakingly created custom playlists with TIDAL and DEX 3 – our [top DJ Software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) solution for the versatile disc jockey. #MixEverything

[Start Playlist Import](https://www.tunemymusic.com/?tidal=true#step1)
